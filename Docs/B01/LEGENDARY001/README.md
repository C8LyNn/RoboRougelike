## Core
- Max **one mounted instance per stable LegendaryDefinitionId PER individual robot**, INCLUDING destroyed 0HP mounted parts. Two distinct definitions mount simultaneously without artificial global caps (though physics, mass, clearance and weapon family capacity constrain).
- Many owned copies of same definition in inventory are legal, provided each has a distinct existing R3PartState.Id. A live owned GUID duplicated across concurrent ownership, pickup, sale, restore and reward is illegal. Preset-template cloning (new instance ID) != save/load (preserve ownership IDs). A second vehicle or enemy follows own per-equipped definition cap.
- This R0 schema describes definition cards only; instance IDs remain in R3. Avoid new enum per legendary kind, avoid global ledger or hard quota. Serialization and atomic CanMount/Commit with no loss of player item are receiver integration responsibilities AFTER B01 G2, not implemented.
- Railgun legendary is counted as Railgun family within future max TWO LMB/RMB families [#41](https://github.com/C8LyNn/RoboRougelike/issues/41); never adds third mouse button or magical bypass. [#140](https://github.com/C8LyNn/RoboRougelike/issues/140) sockets and [#141](https://github.com/C8LyNn/RoboRougelike/issues/141) part sizes remain gating. Heat and energy in sample are *future flags*, not assumed live systems.
