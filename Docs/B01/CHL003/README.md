## Physics, telemetry and same-step finish invariants

A CP[last] + Finish physically co-located in a single FixedUpdate cannot win immediately, regardless of OnTriggerEnter order: `CHL003FinishArbiter` queues contact, waits at least one physics step since both contact and final CP, and checks **actual** `Physics.ComputePenetration` overlap with the authoritative actor. `OnTriggerStay` handles sustained overlap; early finish, another actor, pause, a stale callback or Retry cannot complete. Pause freezes the Rigidbody as kinematic and resume restores saved velocities; Retry resets both actor motion and counters.

`CHL003ContactMonitor` counts original actual `Collision` entry/manifold contacts, broken down into barrier/ramp/bridge/ground; `CHL003TrialMetrics` records elapsed time, physics samples, peak speed, distance, min/max height, CP order, falls, wins and each Light/Heavy attempt. HUD shows measurements and Editor.log/Player.log stores each ended/reset trial's summary/event CSV. The original scene and geometry are procedurally instantiated from the **same CHL demo bootstrap** every time, not a separate route editor or invisible direct-completion harness. G1 real Light/Heavy crossings remain NOT_RUN.
