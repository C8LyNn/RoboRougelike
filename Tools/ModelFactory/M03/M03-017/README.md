**Mount:** chassis socket; no changes to gameplay or shared Unity assets.

## Geometry and assembly

A 0.72 × 0.50 m graphite chassis plate (mounting plane Z=0) carries two open-bore titanium toroidal collars, a 0.48 m sliding pin, copper operating lever, ceramic isolator, two indicator tracks, and four corner mounting pads. The **real cylindrical bolt** translates along +X by 0.10 m to enter the receiver; the bore inner radius is 0.046 m and the bolt radius is 0.029 m (radial clearance 0.017 m). The lever sits outside the guide ring's swept envelope. Both colored indicator tabs slide vertically in their own open channels: **red raised = unlocked; green raised = locked**. No hidden material swaps or simulated 2D holes.

Pivot: (0,0,0) at the bottom chassis mounting plane. Unity scale: **1 Blender unit = 1 metre**. Four empties named M03_017_SNAP_*_Z_UP at (±0.282, ±0.188, 0) with +Z mounting normals. Root empty: M03_017_ROOT_PIVOT_MOUNT_Z0. No rigid-body physics or actual gameplay latch state is implemented; animation is presentation-only.

Six PBR materials: graphite, titanium, copper, ceramic, red, green. Surfaces use metallic/roughness and geometric bevels, not emissive-only cues. Intentional assembly contacts: mounting pads against plate, bolt handle against pin, collar supports against collars. The pin remains inside the ring bores with clearance; indicator tabs stay between their rails.

## Animation (24 fps)

Frames 1 → 24: lock bolt +0.10 m along X, red flag lowers and green flag rises. Frames 24 → 48: locked hold. Frames 48 → 72: return to unlocked. Animation objects: M03_017_ANIM_LOCK_BOLT_PLUS_X, M03_017_ANIM_UNLOCKED_RED_FLAG_Z, M03_017_ANIM_LOCKED_GREEN_FLAG_Z. No Blender constraints or drivers are needed for export.
